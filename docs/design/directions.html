Mockups at phone width (360px) of the registration screen and the success "raffle ticket" screen. Both directions use the official title artwork and the Citizens of Light Church mark. Scroll inside each phone to see more.

Direction A · Recommended

Flyer, Alive

PersonalityLoud, joyful, street-bold, sticker-pasted. It looks exactly like the poster people just saw on WhatsApp Status, now something they can tap.
PaletteCream base, ember-orange starburst, maroon info panels, black ink outlines with hard offset shadows.
creamembere-700maroonink
TypeLilita One (chunky display, echoes "FRESHERS PLUG IN"), plus Barlow Condensed with italic for details (echoes the flyer's venue text). The title is the real artwork.
LayoutThe poster as the first screen: starburst, Day badges, title, venue panel, then the form on cream. Inputs and buttons are "stickers" with a thick black outline and a hard shadow.
MotionTactile and minimal. Buttons physically press into their shadow (150ms). The Day badges rotate slowly. The ticket stamps in (400ms). Everything is off for reduced-motion users.
Signature moments① Rotating DAY 1 / DAY 2 badges lifted straight from the flyer. ② Sticker-press buttons. ③ A raffle ticket with a perforated stub and an "ENTERED!" rubber stamp.

Direction B

Stage Night

PersonalityElectric, premium, concert-ready. It feels like a ticketed show: the night before the big event, not the poster on the wall.
PaletteDeep maroon-black stage, a warm spotlight glow behind the title, ember as the single accent, cream for the form sheet.
stagemaroonembercream
TypeAnton (tall, poster-condensed) for headings and big numbers; Archivo (a variable-width sans) for body and form text. Cleaner and more editorial.
LayoutA dark hero with dates in a ruled two-column strip, and programme chips. The form slides up as a cream sheet with rounded top corners. Inputs are soft filled fields with no outlines.
MotionSmooth and cinematic. The sheet rises in, buttons scale on press, and the wristband ticket fades in at scale. There's no ambient motion. Everything is off for reduced-motion users.
Signature moments① A static spotlight glow behind the title artwork. ② Dates set like a concert listing. ③ An orange "wristband" ticket with a tear-off stub.
