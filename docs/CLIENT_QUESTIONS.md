# The Awakening Registration: Questions for the Church

Hello! Thank you for trusting me with the registration page for The Awakening. Before and during the build, a few decisions are needed from the church.

Each question has a **suggested answer**. If you're not sure, simply reply "go with the suggestion" and I'll use it. The first section affects the build directly, so answers there are most helpful soon.

---

## MUST ANSWER BEFORE WE START

### Raffle draw

**1. On which day will the raffle draw take place: Day 1 (Oct 24), Day 2 (Oct 25), or both?**
*Why it matters:* it decides how long registration should stay open, and when the list of codes must be final.
*Suggested answer:* **Day 2**, near the end of the programme, so the most people are present.

**2. Must winners be present at the venue to claim a prize?**
*Why it matters:* about 500 people may register, but not all will attend. If an absent person's code is drawn, there needs to be a clear rule.
*Suggested answer:* **Yes. Read out the code; if nobody claims it within about a minute, draw again.**

**3. How should we confirm that a winner is the real owner of the code?**
*Why it matters:* codes can be screenshotted and shared, so someone else could claim a prize.
*Suggested answer:* **The winner shows the code on their phone (email or screenshot) and gives their full name and phone number. An organiser checks these against the registration list.**

**4. Can each person register only once?**
*Why it matters:* without this, someone could register several times to get more raffle tickets.
*Suggested answer:* **Yes, one registration per phone number and per email address.**

### Registration period

**5. When should registration close?**
*Why it matters:* the transport team needs a final list in time to plan buses. If people can still register at the venue, the raffle list keeps changing.
*Suggested answer:* **Keep registration open until the raffle draw, so people at the venue can register on their phones. Use the list as of the night of Oct 22 for transport planning.**

### Personal information and consent

**6. Do you approve this short notice for the form?**
*Why it matters:* Nigerian data protection law (NDPA 2023) requires telling people what we collect and why. Being clear also builds trust.
*Suggested wording:*
> "By registering, you agree that Citizens of Light Church may use your details to organise The Awakening (including transport and the raffle draw) and to contact you afterwards by phone, WhatsApp or email about church activities. We will not share your details outside the church. ☐ I agree"

You're welcome to edit it.

**7. Should we add an age confirmation?**
*Why it matters:* some freshers are 16 or 17. Data about under-18s needs extra care under the law.
*Suggested answer:* **Yes, add a checkbox: "I am 18 or older, or I have my parent/guardian's consent."**

### Form content

**8. Which institutions should be listed in the "Institution" field?**
*Why it matters:* a list makes the data clean and easy to count. There will always be an "Other" option with a text box.
*Suggested answer:* **University of Ilorin, Kwara State University, Kwara State Polytechnic, Al-Hikmah University, Other.** Please add or remove as you see fit.

**9. For transport, can we ask "Do you need a pickup bus? Yes/No" and "Area/neighbourhood" alongside the address?**
*Why it matters:* the transport team will choose central pickup points from this data. Short, consistent area names (for example "Tanke", "Oke-Odo", "School hostel") are much easier to group than full addresses.
*Suggested answer:* **Yes, add both. Only show the address and area questions to people who say they need a bus.**

### Church Gmail account

**10. Can the person who manages the church Gmail account turn on 2-Step Verification and create an "app password" for the registration site?**
*Why it matters:* the site sends confirmation emails from the church's own Gmail address. Google only allows this with an app password, and creating one requires 2-Step Verification. It takes about 5 minutes, and I can guide whoever manages the account. The app password can be deleted after the event, which immediately cuts off the site's access.
*Suggested answer:* **Yes. The account manager sets it up with me on a short call, and the app password is deleted after the event.**

## CAN DECIDE LATER (before or around launch)

**11. How will registrants be told their pickup point?**
*Why it matters:* pickup points are chosen after registration, so the confirmation email can't include them.
*Suggested answer:* **A WhatsApp broadcast or group message to people who need transport, sent by Oct 22.** The church's team would handle this using the registration Sheet.

**12. Who should have access to the registration Sheet?**
*Why it matters:* the Sheet holds students' phone numbers and addresses, so access should be limited.
*Suggested answer:* **The event coordinator, the transport lead and the follow-up lead only, with view or edit access as needed.**

**13. Who should own the website hosting account?**
*Why it matters:* the site will run on Vercel's free plan. If it lives only on my personal account, the church depends on me to keep it running.
*Suggested answer:* **I create it under an account the church controls (using the church Gmail), and the church gives me access.**

**14. What should the confirmation email say, and who signs it off?**
*Why it matters:* it's the first message many freshers will get from the church.
*Suggested answer:* **A warm welcome, their raffle code in large text, the event dates, times and venue, and "See you there!" from the Citizens of Light Church team.** I'll draft it for your approval.

**15. What happens to the page and data after the event?**
*Why it matters:* closing it properly protects people's data and prevents late sign-ups.
*Suggested answer:* **Close the form after the draw, showing a "Thank you, registration is closed" page. Keep the Sheet for follow-up and membership, and review it after 12 months.**

---

## Something you should know

The church's Gmail account is free, and free Gmail limits how many emails can be sent per day. On busy days, such as right after campus outreach, some confirmation emails may arrive a few hours late. **Every registrant sees their raffle code on screen immediately and is asked to screenshot it**, so nobody loses their code. If you'd like to avoid any delay in future, a church website domain (a small yearly cost) would allow a dedicated email service.

Thank you! 🙏
